Ek ghazal Saim ki taraf se Farya Sheikh k naam
Teri ankho me jo dekha, woh khuwab haseen tha, Mere dil k har ek kone me tera hi naam tha.
Teri muskurahat se roshan meri har ek raat hai, Tere hone se hi meri poori duniya aabad hai.
Teri ankho ka noor chand se kam nahi, Teri haseen muskurahat se pyara kuch bhi nahi.
Mai tujhe lafzo me kese bayan karu, Tu woh ehsaas hai jise mai har dua me yaad karu.
Tere begair har khushi adhuri si lagti hai, Teri mojoodgi har gham ko bhula deti hai.
Teri yaado me guzarta hai mera har lamha, Tujhe soch kar muskurata hai mera dil har lamha.
Kash taqdeer hume ek sath le aaye, Aur zindagi hume ek dusre ka humsafar banaye.
Na fasla ho darmiyan, na koi majboori rahe, Tere mere darmiyan bas waado ki roshni rahe.
Mai har mushkil me tera hath thamna chahu, Teri khushi k liye har dua mangu.
Na chand ki khwahish hai, na sitaro ki talab, Mujhe bas teri muskurahat se hai matlab.
Teri peshani pe sajde ka noor hamesha rahe, Teri zindagi me khushi ka mausam hamesha rahe.
Mai waada karta hu, tujhe izzat se chahu ga, Teri khushi me apni khushi dhundu ga.
Agar kabhi meri khamoshi tujhe udaas kare, To samajh lena mera dil tujhe yaad kare.
✦ ♡ ✦
Saim k dil se Farya Sheikh k liye
Tu meri zindagi ka woh haseen khuwab hai, Jise mai har roz apni dua me mangu.
Tu meri chahat, mera yaqeen, mera pyara ehsaas hai, Meri har adhuri ghazal ka tu hi aakhri alfaaz hai.
Na jaane taqdeer me kya likha hai mere liye, Magar meri har dua me ek hi naam hai — Farya Sheikh.
Tum meri woh dua ho jise mai hamesha mangu, Tum meri woh khushi ho jise mai kabhi khona na chahu. ❤️
